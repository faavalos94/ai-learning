## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per year, plus 10 company holidays. Vacation days accrue monthly, and up to 5 unused days roll over to the next year. Request time off in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.

## Sick Leave
Employees get 10 paid sick days per year. You don't need a doctor's note for absences of 3 days or less. Sick days do not roll over.

## Remote Work
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require VP approval. Core collaboration hours are 10am to 3pm Pacific.

## Equipment
New hires receive a MacBook Pro and a $500 stipend for home office furniture. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Report lost or stolen devices to IT within 24 hours.

## Expenses
Submit expense reports within 30 days through the finance portal. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Purchases over $1,000 need pre-approval.

## Professional Development
Each employee has an annual $2,000 learning budget for courses, books, and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.
